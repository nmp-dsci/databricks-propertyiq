transcript-rag-agent: zero Databricks code — but your strongest raw material

Finding: a full-tree search found no Databricks platform usage at all — no workspace auth, no Vector Search, no UC, no serving, no Spark. The "Databricks chunks" are corpus content: the Databricks YouTube channel is the largest source in the transcript corpus (8 videos), and GraphRAG clustered those chunks into their own "unity catalog / MLflow / LLMOps" community. The only Databricks package is databricks-sdk pulled in transitively by mlflow-skinny, never imported.

What it is: transcript·lab, an evaluation-first RAG workbench — LangChain/LangGraph, ChromaDB, local MiniLM embeddings, cross-encoder rerank, BM25 + RRF fusion, HyDE/multi-query, GraphRAG on Neo4j with Leiden communities, and a serious eval stack: a chunk-level golden dataset, deterministic IR metrics (recall@k, MRR, NDCG), an 8-config ablation sweep, RAGAS + a custom depth rubric, and CI eval-regression gates.

Why this matters for the interview: every hard, transferable part of RAG — chunking strategy, retrieval fusion, reranking, golden-set evaluation — already exists and is battle-tested. What's missing is the Databricks-native substrate: Delta tables, Vector Search indexes, foundation-model embeddings, UC governance, and serving. That gap is a porting exercise, not a research project — and porting it is project 1 below.
